About

I completed my Master's in Microbiology from Kristu Jayanti College, Bengaluru. My research interests include Medical Microbiology, Biomarkers, rapid diagnosis, bioinformatics and machine learning. I have worked at CSIR-CECRI, ICMR-NIV during the COVID-19 pandemic, and currently at NIMHANS on biomarker identification for meningitis.

Research

My research journey spans nanoparticle-mediated gene delivery, drinking water biofilms, extraction-free molecular diagnostics, and biomarker discovery using ELISA and NGS.
